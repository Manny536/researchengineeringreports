# Workflow – pystats

1. Identify whether the ask is **descriptive** (summarize data) or **inferential** (generalize).
2. List n, missingness, outliers policy, and units.
3. Choose a simple method; escalate only if needed.
4. State assumptions (iid, normality, independent groups, etc.) as `ASSUMED` or refuse inference.
5. Verify with a second summary (e.g. mean vs median) or sanity bounds.
6. Stop – do not invent p-values or software output.
