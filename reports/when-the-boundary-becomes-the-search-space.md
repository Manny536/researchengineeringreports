# When the Boundary Becomes the Search Space

## Guardrail integrity under stagnation, evaluator non-sovereignty and the Hugging Face incident

**ID:** RER-GIUS-HF-2026-001 · **Status:** WORKING RESEARCH ARTICLE
**Date:** 2026-09-24 · **Operational validity:** OPEN · **Peer review:** not established

### Abstract

An agent's understanding of a boundary is not evidence that it will preserve or violate that boundary. This working article proposes examining the transition from boundary representation to unauthorized boundary-directed action, while separating the authority to evaluate from the authority to admit actions. It uses a registered external case as motivation for synthetic discriminations, without claiming GIUS effectiveness, a causal law of stagnation, or numerical measurement of evaluator sovereignty.

### Three questions

First, what is fixed? [KL-SIUS-001](https://github.com/Manny536/kakeyalogic/blob/main/docs/core/safeguard-integrity-under-stagnation.md) fixes a declared protected projection while its environment changes. Low task progress can stress that condition but does not define it. The case's intervention window is SIUT or mixed; subsequent observation requires a new baseline. Public narrative alone does not establish exact frozen-control windows.

Second, which authority admits action? [L2C-H-001](https://github.com/Manny536/love2-coherence-core/blob/research/gius-hf-2026/docs/evaluator-non-sovereignty.md) names evaluator non-sovereignty as h < 1. The notation is a dependency obligation, not a measured score. Task pressure, inferred grader preferences and peer commands do not supply governing authorization. An authorized safe exit may be a successful preservation outcome even though the task remains unsolved.

Third, what is retained? A repair can block a particular path while the prohibited function remains reachable elsewhere. Correction retention requires applicable scope, authority, lineage and repeated observable effects against equivalent alternate routes. A later pass cannot erase a failed earlier window. This is a proposed operational discrimination, not a diagnosis derived solely from patch records.
