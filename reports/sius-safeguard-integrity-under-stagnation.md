## Abstract

SIUS studies passive decoupling: a safeguard remains declared while its operational environment changes and its force can diminish. This report registers the supplied NotebookLM synthesis as a working research source, extracts its standalone preservation problem, and separates definition, evaluation design, synthetic calibration and empirical claims. It does not establish a universal theorem or validated deployment outcome.

## Standalone basis

SIUT asks about preservation across transformation $X_0\xrightarrow{T}X_1$. SIUS asks about fixed declared controls $X_t=X_0$ under changing $E_t$. They are sibling conditions. The protected projection must be explicit: refactors or retraining that alter it are transformation or mixed cases, not strict stagnation cases merely because policy wording stayed the same.

The source distinguishes **Static Safeguard ≠ Operative Safeguard** and gives the five-coordinate state $\Gamma_G=(s,a,v,e,r)^T$. Its margin is $\Delta_G(t)=\operatorname{ContainmentBoundary}(X_0)-E_{\mathrm{cap}}(t)$, with a Stagnation Boundary Error at $\Delta_G(t)<0$. Numeric use requires calibrated comparable quantities. The controlling repository definition makes this requirement explicit; the source alone supplies no measurement protocol.

## Operational outcomes

Semantic decay loses effective meaning under vocabulary/context drift. Authority stagnation leaves credentials or write permissions operative after their justification changes. Visibility decay misses new downstream activity. Containment decoupling allows capability beyond the modeled boundary. Retention decay leaves corrections in history while their behavioral effect disappears. These are testable failure categories, not measured prevalence estimates.

The source's SAVER sequence is Observe → Grain → Gate → Overlap → Refine → Route → Receipt → Retain. Its retention-regression and route-versus-containment examples motivate controls; historical SAVER receipts do not establish current operational SIUS validity. EEV4 evaluates HELD custody with instructions, evidence, authority and constraints separately typed. `h < 1` expresses evaluator non-sovereignty; longitudinal retention requires correction replay evidence, not a numeric label alone.

## Study path and falsifiers

Declare the fixed projection, environment changes, comparable margin scale, required directions and checkpoint schedule. Evaluate each grain non-compensatorily, verify authority independently, test modeled reachable states beyond planned routes, and replay applicable corrections longitudinally. Preserve negative evidence. A witnessed required-grain failure, lost correction, negative margin or egress leak defeats a scoped preservation claim. A pass remains limited to the tested model and window; independent evaluation is required for promotion.

The separately proposed operator may eventually help localize changes across grains and scales. It does not define SIUS, and neither line segments nor grain vectors are automatically differentiable transport fields. No Kakeya-to-operational theorem transfer is asserted.
